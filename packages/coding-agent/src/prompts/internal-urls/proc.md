proc://: active jobs, agents, and services; proc://<id> shows status and output; writing empty `content` to proc://<id>/kill cancels or terminates jobs, agents, and services; proc://<id>/streams reads a bash job's pipes and redirected output files so far (including live output behind `| tail` or `| grep` or `> log`—never re-run a command just to inspect it); a parked agent or missing job does not mean lost state—inspect agent://<id> and history://<id>.
